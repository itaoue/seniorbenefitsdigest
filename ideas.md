**Idea 1: Authoritative Editorial / Financial Broadsheet**

- **Design Movement**: American Editorial Modernism — inspired by trusted financial publications like The Wall Street Journal and AARP Magazine
- **Core Principles**: Trust through authority, warm accessibility, editorial gravitas, clear information hierarchy
- **Color Philosophy**: Deep navy (#1B2E5A) as the primary authority color, warm amber/gold (#D4A017) as the accent for CTAs and highlights, cream (#F8F4E8) for section backgrounds to evoke newspaper warmth, white for clean content areas. - **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric editorial grid — hero section uses a 60/40 split with large serif headline left and image right; stats bar spans full width; content sections alternate between left-heavy and right-heavy layouts. No generic centered boxes.
- **Signature Elements**: (1) Thick horizontal rule dividers in amber gold; (2) Numbered category badges in navy circles; (3) Subtle paper-texture background on cream sections
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Deliberate, unhurried interactions — hover states use gentle underlines and color shifts rather than dramatic transforms. Forms feel trustworthy with clear labels.
- **Animation**: Fade-in-up on scroll for content cards; counter animation for stats; no jarring transitions
- **Typography System**: Playfair Display (serif) for headings — conveys authority and tradition; Source Sans Pro for body — highly readable at all sizes; Playfair Display Italic for accent text like "$22K+" figures

## Selected Design: Idea 1 — Authoritative Editorial / Financial Broadsheet

**Chosen design philosophy**: Authoritative Editorial Modernism with deep navy authority, warm amber accents, and cream editorial backgrounds. This approach mirrors the trustworthiness of established financial publications while remaining warm and accessible to seniors. The Playfair Display serif typography conveys gravitas and credibility, while the asymmetric editorial layout avoids generic AI-slop centered grids.
